📊 Power BI Capstone Project: E-commerce Sales Dashboard

This project presents a comprehensive sales performance analysis for an e-commerce retail company. Using Power BI, I designed an interactive dashboard to uncover key business insights that support data-driven decision-making.
🔍 Key Insights:
💰 Total Revenue: ₦8.91 million
📦 Total Orders: 397,880
👥 Total Customers: 4,338


📈 Monthly Revenue Trend:
Revenue steadily increased over the year, peaking in November (₦1.16M), followed by December (₦1.09M) and October (₦1.04M) — indicating strong Q4 sales performance, likely due to holiday shopping patterns.
🛒 Top 5 Products by Revenue:
1. Paper Craft, Little Birdie – ₦168K

2. Regency Cake Stand (3 Tier) – ₦143K

3. White Hanging Heart T-Light Holder – ₦100K

4. Jumbo Bag Red Retrosot – ₦85K

5. Medium Ceramic Top Storage Jar – ₦81K
These products contributed significantly to overall sales, highlighting the best-performing SKUs in the inventory.

👤 Customer Segmentation:
High Spenders: 6 customers → ₦312.99K from 7 orders
Low Spenders: 4,337 customers → ₦8.60M from 397K+ orders


This indicates a broad but low-spend customer base, suggesting opportunities to increase customer lifetime value through loyalty programs or product bundling.
